GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions.

Can I buy a real ticket here?

No. Eventify is a student demo. The events and prices are examples, and the booking form does not reserve tickets.

How do I find the price of an event?

Open the Events page. Each event has a price per person, and there is a table to help you compare them.

Can I book for a group?

The example form lets you choose one to five tickets. A real service would need a booking system to check places and take payments.

What if I need step-free access?

For a real event, check access details directly with the venue before paying. The demo form includes a space to try adding access requests.
