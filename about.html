THE IDEA BEHIND EVENTIFY

More time enjoying.
Less time searching.

Choosing something to do should be exciting. Eventify helps people find new experiences with clear prices, simple event details and an easy menu.

I built this website as a computing project to practise HTML and CSS. My aim was to make a website that feels useful and is easy to understand.

It is designed for people looking for a day out, including students, friends and anyone who wants to try something new.
